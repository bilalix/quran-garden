> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Hujurat, Verse 4 (49:4) - Translation
> Indeed, most of those who call out to you ˹O Prophet˺ from outside ˹your˺ private quarters have no understanding ˹of manners˺.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: A group from the tribe of Bani Tamîm came to see the Prophet (ﷺ) while he was taking a nap in the afternoon. They stood outside the homes of his wives and started calling him out loud.

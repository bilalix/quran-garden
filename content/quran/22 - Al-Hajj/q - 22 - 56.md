> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Hajj, Verse 56 (22:56) - Translation
> All authority on that Day is for Allah ˹alone˺.[^1] He will judge between them. So those who believe and do good will be in the Gardens of Bliss.
^translation

[^1]: Allah grants authority to some of His servants in this world, but none will have authority on Judgment Day except Him. See [[q - 3 - 26|3:26]].

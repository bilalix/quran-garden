> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Hajj, Verse 39 (22:39) - Translation
> Permission ˹to fight back˺ is ˹hereby˺ granted to those being fought, for they have been wronged.[^1] And Allah is truly Most Capable of helping them ˹prevail˺.
^translation

[^1]: For over thirteen years, Muslims had not been allowed to fight back against the brutality of the Meccan pagans, leading to the emigration of the Prophet and many of his companions to Medina. As the hostilities continued, this verse was later revealed allowing Muslims to fight back in self-defence.

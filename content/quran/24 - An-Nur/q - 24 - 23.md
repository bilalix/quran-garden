> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nur, Verse 23 (24:23) - Translation
> Surely those who accuse chaste, unsuspecting,[^1] believing women are cursed in this life and the Hereafter. And they will suffer a tremendous punishment
^translation

[^1]: Immoral thoughts never cross the minds of these chaste women and, therefore, they never expect slander.

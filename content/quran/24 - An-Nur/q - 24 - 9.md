> <span class="quran-arabic">وَٱلْخَـٰمِسَةَ أَنَّ غَضَبَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْهَآ إِن كَانَ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰدِقِينَ</span>

> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nur, Verse 9 (24:9) - Translation
> and a fifth oath that Allah may be displeased with her if he is telling the truth.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: This ruling is called li’ân in Islamic legal system. Once the husband and the wife swear each five times, as specified in [24:6-9](/24:6-9), the marriage is terminated forever—which means that they can never be remarried.

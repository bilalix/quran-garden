> [!translation]+ Surah Nuh, Verse 4 (71:4) - Translation
> He will forgive your sins, and delay your end until the appointed time.[^1] Indeed, when the time set by Allah comes, it cannot be delayed, if only you knew!”
^translation

[^1]: Meaning, it is out of Allah’s mercy that He does not hasten your punishment but allows you more time to repent.

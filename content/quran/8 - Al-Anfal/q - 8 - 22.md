> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Anfal, Verse 22 (8:22) - Translation
> Indeed, the worst of all beings in the sight of Allah are the ˹wilfully˺ deaf and dumb, who do not understand.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: This refers metaphorically to the disbelievers who fail to see and hear the truth.

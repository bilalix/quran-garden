> [!translation]+ Surah An-Naml, Verse 7 (27:7) - Translation
> ˹Remember˺ when Moses said to his family, “I have spotted a fire. I will either bring you some directions[^1] from there, or a burning torch so you may warm yourselves.”
^translation

[^1]: lit., information. Moses and his family lost their way in the dark while they were travelling from Midian to Egypt.

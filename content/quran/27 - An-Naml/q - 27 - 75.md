> [!translation]+ Surah An-Naml, Verse 75 (27:75) - Translation
> For there is nothing hidden in the heavens or the earth without being ˹written˺ in a perfect Record.[^1] 
^translation

[^1]: The Record refers to the Preserved Tablet (Al-Lawḥ Al-Maḥfûẓ) in which Allah has written the destiny of His entire creation.

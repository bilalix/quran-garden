> [!translation]+ Surah An-Naml, Verse 15 (27:15) - Translation
> Indeed, We granted knowledge to David and Solomon. And they said ˹in acknowledgment˺, “All praise is for Allah Who has privileged us over many of His faithful servants.”[^1]
^translation

[^1]: The privilege was their ability to communicate with members of the animal kingdom, control the wind, utilize the jinn for their service, etc.

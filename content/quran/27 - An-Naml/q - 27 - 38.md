> [!translation]+ Surah An-Naml, Verse 38 (27:38) - Translation
> Solomon asked, “O chiefs! Which of you can bring me her throne before they come to me in ˹full˺ submission?”[^1]
^translation

[^1]: To explain why Prophet Solomon brought the Queen’s magnificent throne and built the marvellous palace, many traditional commentators cite some folkloric or legendary stories. However, none of these stories is supported by reliable Islamic sources.

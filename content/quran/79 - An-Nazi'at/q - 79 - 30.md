> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nazi'at, Verse 30 (79:30) - Translation
> As for the earth, He spread it out as well,[^1]
^translation

[^1]: The Arabic verb daḥa also suggests that the earth is egg-shaped. The noun form diḥya is still used in some Arab dialects to mean an egg. Based on [[q - 2 - 29|2:29]], [41:9-12](/41:9-12), and [79:27-33](/79:27-33), Allah ordained both realms, then formed the earth and created its provisions, then formed the heaven into seven heavens—all in six ˹heavenly˺ Days. See footnote for [7:54](/7:54).

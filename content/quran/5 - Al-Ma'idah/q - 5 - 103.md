> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Ma'idah, Verse 103 (5:103) - Translation
> Allah has never ordained the ˹so-called˺ baḥîrah, sâ'ibah, waṣîlah, and ḥâm camels.[^1] But the disbelievers[^2] just fabricate lies about Allah, and most of them lack understanding.
^translation

[^1]: These camels were allowed by pagan Arabs to pasture freely and were not to be used for labour or transportation after fathering or giving birth to a certain number of male or female camels.
[^2]: i.e., the pagans.

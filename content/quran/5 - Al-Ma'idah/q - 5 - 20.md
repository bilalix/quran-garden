> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Ma'idah, Verse 20 (5:20) - Translation
> And ˹remember˺ when Moses said to his people, “O my people! Remember Allah’s favours upon you when He raised prophets from among you, made you sovereign,[^1] and gave you what He had never given anyone in the world.[^2]
^translation

[^1]: He rescued you from Egypt where the Pharaoh treated you like slaves and blessed you with freedom so you can manage your own affairs.
[^2]: i.e., the miracles of splitting the sea, water coming out of the rock, manna and quails, and clouds shading them.

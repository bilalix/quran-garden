> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Ma'idah, Verse 60 (5:60) - Translation
> Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Shall I inform you of those who deserve a worse punishment from Allah ˹than the rebellious˺? It is those who earned Allah’s condemnation and displeasure—some being reduced to apes and pigs[^1] and worshippers of false gods. These are far worse in rank and farther astray from the Right Way.”
^translation

[^1]: Literally or metaphorically. See footnote for [[q - 2 - 65|2:65]].

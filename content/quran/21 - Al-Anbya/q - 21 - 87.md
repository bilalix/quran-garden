> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Anbya, Verse 87 (21:87) - Translation
> And ˹remember˺ when the Man of the Whale stormed off ˹from his city˺ in a rage, thinking We would not restrain him.[^1] Then in the ˹veils of˺ darkness[^2] he cried out, “There is no god ˹worthy of worship˺ except You. Glory be to You! I have certainly done wrong.”
^translation

[^1]: Or “thinking We will let him get away with it.” Jonah (ﷺ) had been met with denial for many years. When he sensed the coming of Allah’s torment, he abandoned his city without Allah’s permission. Eventually, his people repented before the coming of the torment, and Allah accepted their repentance (see [[q - 10 - 98|10:98]]), whereas Jonah ended up in the belly of the whale (see [37:140-148](/37:140-148)).
[^2]: The darkness of the night, the sea, and the belly of the whale.

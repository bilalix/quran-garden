> [!translation]+ Surah Fatir, Verse 24 (35:24) - Translation
> We have surely sent you with the truth as a deliverer of good news and a warner. There is no community that has not had a warner.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: According to a Prophetic narration collected by Ibn Ḥibbân, the total number of prophets sent around the world, from Adam (ﷺ) to Muḥammad (ﷺ), is 124 000—of which only twenty-five are mentioned in the Quran.

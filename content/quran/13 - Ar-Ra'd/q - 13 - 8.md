> [!translation]+ Surah Ar-Ra'd, Verse 8 (13:8) - Translation
> Allah knows what every female bears and what increases and decreases in the wombs.[^1] And with Him everything is determined with precision.
^translation

[^1]: He knows whether the egg will be fertilized or not, whether the baby will be born before or after nine months, whether the pregnancy will end with delivery or miscarriage, and whether there will be one baby or more.

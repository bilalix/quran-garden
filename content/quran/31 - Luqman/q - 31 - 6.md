> [!translation]+ Surah Luqman, Verse 6 (31:6) - Translation
> But there are some who employ theatrics,[^1] only to lead others away from Allah’s Way—without any knowledge—and to make a mockery of it. They will suffer a humiliating punishment.
^translation

[^1]: Some pagan Arabs used to utilize singing, dancing, chanting, etc. to distract people’s attention from listening to the recitation of the Quran.

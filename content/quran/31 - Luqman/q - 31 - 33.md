> [!translation]+ Surah Luqman, Verse 33 (31:33) - Translation
> O humanity! Be mindful of your Lord, and beware of a Day when no parent will be of any benefit to their child, nor will a child be of any benefit to their parent. Surely Allah’s promise is true. So do not let the life of this world deceive you, nor let the Chief Deceiver[^1] deceive you about Allah.
^translation

[^1]: Satan.

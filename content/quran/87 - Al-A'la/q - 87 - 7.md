> [!translation]+ Surah Al-A'la, Verse 7 (87:7) - Translation
> unless Allah wills otherwise.[^1] He surely knows what is open and what is hidden.
^translation

[^1]: Meaning, We will have you memorize the revelations and apply the rulings contained in them, unless one ruling is replaced by another.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Ahzab, Verse 69 (33:69) - Translation
> O believers! Do not be like those who slandered Moses, but Allah cleared him of what they said. And he was honourable in the sight of Allah.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: The Quran does not specify how Moses was slandered by some of his people. Some narrations suggest that he was either falsely accused of adultery (see footnote for [[q - 28 - 76|28:76]]), killing his brother Aaron (who died a natural death), or having a skin disease since he, unlike others, used to bathe with his clothes on.

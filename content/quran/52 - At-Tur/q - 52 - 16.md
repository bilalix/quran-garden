> [!translation]+ Surah At-Tur, Verse 16 (52:16) - Translation
> Burn in it! It is the same whether you endure ˹it˺ patiently or not.[^1] You are only rewarded for what you used to do.”
^translation

[^1]: One gets the reward for being patient in difficult times in this world. As for the Hereafter, those who suffer patiently in Hell will not be rewarded for their patience.

> [!translation]+ Surah At-Tur, Verse 32 (52:32) - Translation
> Or do their ˹intelligent˺ minds prompt them to this ˹paradox˺?[^1] Or are they ˹just˺ a transgressing people?
^translation

[^1]: The pagan leaders claimed to be Mecca’s most intelligent minds. So this verse questions their intelligence, arguing that the Prophet (ﷺ) cannot be a madman and a poet at the same time.

> [!translation]+ Surah Yunus, Verse 47 (10:47) - Translation
> And for every community there is a messenger. After their messenger has come,[^1] judgment is passed on them in all fairness, and they are not wronged.
^translation

[^1]: Either in this life to deliver the message or on the Day of Judgment to testify for or against them.

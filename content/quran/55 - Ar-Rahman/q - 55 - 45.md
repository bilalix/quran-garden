> [!translation]+ Surah Ar-Rahman, Verse 45 (55:45) - Translation
> Then which of your Lord’s favours will you both deny?[^1] 
^translation

[^1]: Warning of the punishment in Hell is listed as a favour just like the promise of the reward in Paradise. Think of this as a road sign: the latter is like a sign about food, gas, etc., and the former is like a cautionary sign about an accident ahead or something hazardous. Both signs benefit people.

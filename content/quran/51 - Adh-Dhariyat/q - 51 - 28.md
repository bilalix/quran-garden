> [!translation]+ Surah Adh-Dhariyat, Verse 28 (51:28) - Translation
> ˹They did not eat,˺ so he grew fearful of them.[^1] They reassured ˹him˺, “Do not be afraid,” and gave him good news of a knowledgeable son.[^2]
^translation

[^1]: As mentioned in [11:69-70](/11:69-70), the angels came to Abraham in the form of men and when he offered them food, they did not eat. In ancient Middle Eastern culture, if a guest refused to eat the food provided by their host, it was a sign of ill-will.
[^2]: Prophet Isaac (ﷺ).

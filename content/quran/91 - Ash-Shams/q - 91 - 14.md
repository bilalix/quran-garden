> Still they defied him and slaughtered her. So their Lord crushed them for their crime, levelling all to the ground.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: It is true that while Qudâr ibn Sâlif was the individual who killed the camel, the rest of the people were supportive of what he did, so they were all punished collectively.

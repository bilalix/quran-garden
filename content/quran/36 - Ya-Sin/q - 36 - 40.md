> [!translation]+ Surah Ya-Sin, Verse 40 (36:40) - Translation
> It is not for the sun to catch up with the moon,[^1] nor does the night outrun the day. Each is travelling in an orbit of their own.
^translation

[^1]: The moon completes a cycle every month (waxing and waning), while the sun takes a whole year to complete its cycle (resulting in the spring, summer, fall, and winter seasons).

> [!translation]+ Surah Ya-Sin, Verse 80 (36:80) - Translation
> ˹He is the One˺ Who gives you fire from green trees, and—behold!—you kindle ˹fire˺ from them.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: The verse refers to two types of Arabian trees, markh and ’afâr, which produce fire when their green branches are rubbed together.

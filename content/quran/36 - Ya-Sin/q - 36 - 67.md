> [!translation]+ Surah Ya-Sin, Verse 67 (36:67) - Translation
> And had We willed, We could have transfigured them on the spot,[^1] so they could neither progress forward nor turn back.
^translation

[^1]: Meaning, We have the ability to turn them into stone or any other inanimate object.

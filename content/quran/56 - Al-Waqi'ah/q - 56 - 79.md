> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Waqi'ah, Verse 79 (56:79) - Translation
> touched by none except the purified ˹angels˺.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: This verse refutes the pagan claim that the Quran was sent down by devils (see [26:210-212](/26:210-212)). Based on this verse, many jurists maintain that a Muslim should perform ablutions before reading the Quran.

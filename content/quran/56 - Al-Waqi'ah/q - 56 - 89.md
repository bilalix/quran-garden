> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Waqi'ah, Verse 89 (56:89) - Translation
> then ˹such a person will have˺ serenity, fragrance,[^1] and a Garden of Bliss.
^translation

[^1]: lit., fragrant basil. Throughout the Quran, Paradise is described as being pleasant to the eye, the heart, and the ear. This verse emphasizes the scent as well.

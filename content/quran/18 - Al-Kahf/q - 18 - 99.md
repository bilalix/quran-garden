> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Kahf, Verse 99 (18:99) - Translation
> On that Day, We will let them[^1] surge ˹like waves˺ over one another. Later, the Trumpet will be blown,[^2] and We will gather all ˹people˺ together.
^translation

[^1]: Gog and Magog.
[^2]: On the Day of Judgment, the Trumpet will be blown by an angel—causing all to die. When it is blown a second time, everyone will be raised from the dead to be assembled for the Judgment (see [[q - 39 - 68|39:68]]).

> [!translation]+ Surah As-Saffat, Verse 149 (37:149) - Translation
> Ask them ˹O Prophet˺ if your Lord has daughters,[^1] while the pagans ˹prefer to˺ have sons.
^translation

[^1]: Some pagan Arabs believed that the angels were Allah’s daughters. Some of them were ashamed to have daughters and took pride in their own sons.

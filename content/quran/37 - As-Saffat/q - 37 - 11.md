> [!translation]+ Surah As-Saffat, Verse 11 (37:11) - Translation
> So ask them ˹O Prophet˺, which is harder to create: them or other marvels of Our creation?[^1] Indeed, We created them from a sticky clay.[^2]
^translation

[^1]: i.e., the angels, the sun, the moon, the stars, and the mountains.
[^2]: Their father Adam (ﷺ).

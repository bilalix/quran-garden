> [!translation]+ Surah As-Saffat, Verse 112 (37:112) - Translation
> We ˹later˺ gave him good news of Isaac—a prophet, and one of the righteous.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: The birth of Isaac is announced after the story of sacrifice. So this is a proof that the son who was going to be sacrificed was Ishmael, not Isaac. This is also supported by [[q - 11 - 71|11:71]], where Sarah is told that she would give birth to Isaac, who would (reach adulthood and) have a son by the name of Jacob.

> [!translation]+ Surah Taha, Verse 28 (20:28) - Translation
> so people may understand my speech,[^1]
^translation

[^1]: Moses (ﷺ) put a brand of fire in his mouth, which hindered his speech as he grew up. In this verse, he prays to Allah to help him speak clearly, and his prayer is answered.

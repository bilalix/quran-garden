> [!translation]+ Surah Taha, Verse 114 (20:114) - Translation
> Exalted is Allah, the True King! Do not rush to recite ˹a revelation of˺ the Quran ˹O Prophet˺ before it is ˹properly˺ conveyed to you,[^1] and pray, “My Lord! Increase me in knowledge.”
^translation

[^1]: The Prophet (ﷺ) was eager to recite the Quran while it was being revealed to him through the angel Gabriel. So he (ﷺ) was told to take his time to learn it by heart once the verses are properly delivered to him.

> [!translation]+ Surah Taha, Verse 133 (20:133) - Translation
> They demand, “If only he could bring us a sign from his Lord!”[^1] Have they not ˹already˺ received a confirmation of what is in earlier Scriptures?[^2]
^translation

[^1]: The pagans of Mecca were not satisfied with a literary miracle in the form of the Quran, so they asked for a tangible miracle (see [17:90-91](/17:90-91)) similar to the staff of Moses.
[^2]: This either refers to the Biblical stories confirmed by the Quran or the prophecies of Muḥammad (ﷺ) in the Bible. Some Muslim scholars cite Deuteronomy [[q - 18 - 2|18:2]] and [33:2](/33:2), Isaiah 42, and John [14:16](/14:16) as examples of the description of Prophet Muḥammad (ﷺ) in the Bible. However, Bible scholars interpret these verses differently.

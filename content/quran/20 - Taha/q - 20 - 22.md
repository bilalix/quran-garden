> [!translation]+ Surah Taha, Verse 22 (20:22) - Translation
> And put your hand under your armpit, it will come out ˹shining˺ white, unblemished,[^1] as another sign,
^translation

[^1]: Moses, who was dark-skinned, was asked to put his hand under his armpit. When he took it out it was shining white, but not out of a skin condition like melanoma.

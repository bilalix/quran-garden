> [!translation]+ Surah Taha, Verse 80 (20:80) - Translation
> O Children of Israel! We saved you from your enemy, and made an appointment with you[^1] on the right side of Mount Ṭûr, and sent down to you manna and quails,[^2]
^translation

[^1]: i.e., your prophet to receive the Tablets for your guidance.
[^2]: Manna (heavenly bread) and quails (chicken-like birds) sustained the children of Israel in the wilderness after they left Egypt.

> [!translation]+ Surah At-Tawbah, Verse 27 (9:27) - Translation
> Then afterwards Allah will turn in grace to whoever He wills.[^1] And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.
^translation

[^1]: i.e., Allah will accept those who embrace Islam. Almost all of Arabia became Muslim before the death of Prophet Muḥammad (ﷺ) in 11 A.H./632 C.E.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-'Ankabut, Verse 24 (29:24) - Translation
> But the only response of Abraham’s people was to say: “Kill him or burn him!” But Allah saved him from the fire.[^1] Surely in this are signs for people who believe.
^translation

[^1]: The idolaters tried to burn Abraham because he smashed their idols. See [21:51-71](/21:51-71) for the full story.

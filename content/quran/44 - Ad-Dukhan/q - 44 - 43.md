> Surely ˹the fruit of˺ the tree of Zaqqûm[^1]

[^1]: Zaqqûm is a tree that grows in the depths of Hell as mentioned in [37:62-65](/37:62-65). The pagans of Mecca used to make fun of the Prophet and say, “How can a tree grow in Hell?”

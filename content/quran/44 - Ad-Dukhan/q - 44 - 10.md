> [!translation]+ Surah Ad-Dukhan, Verse 10 (44:10) - Translation
> Wait then ˹O Prophet˺ for the day ˹when˺ the sky will be veiled in haze,[^1] clearly visible,
^translation

[^1]: This refers to the drought that affected the Meccan pagans so badly that they came to the Prophet (ﷺ), begging him to pray to Allah to remove the affliction from them.

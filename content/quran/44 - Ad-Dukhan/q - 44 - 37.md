> [!translation]+ Surah Ad-Dukhan, Verse 37 (44:37) - Translation
> Are they superior to the people of Tubba’[^1] and those before them? We destroyed them ˹all˺, ˹for˺ they were truly wicked.
^translation

[^1]: Tubba’ Al-Ḥimiari was an ancient righteous Yemeni king whose people persisted in disbelief and were destroyed, although they were superior to Meccans in strength and manpower.

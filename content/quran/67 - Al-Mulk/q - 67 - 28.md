> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Mulk, Verse 28 (67:28) - Translation
> Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Consider this: whether Allah causes me and those with me to die or shows us mercy, who will save the disbelievers from a painful punishment?”[^1]
^translation

[^1]: Meaning, whether Allah takes our lives (as you wish) or grants us a long life, it is all good for us. But who will protect you from Allah if you disbelieve in Him.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Mulk, Verse 3 (67:3) - Translation
> ˹He is the One˺ Who created seven heavens, one above the other. You will never see any imperfection in the creation of the Most Compassionate.[^1] So look again: do you see any flaws?
^translation

[^1]: Some may point to birth defects, genetic mutations, etc. and say these are flaws. This verse means that, in the grand scheme of creation, everything created has its place, form, and purpose, and that it does exactly what Allah ordained for it.

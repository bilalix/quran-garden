> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Mulk, Verse 12 (67:12) - Translation
> Indeed, those in awe of their Lord without seeing Him[^1] will have forgiveness and a mighty reward.
^translation

[^1]: This can also mean that they are in awe of their Lord as much in private as they are in public.

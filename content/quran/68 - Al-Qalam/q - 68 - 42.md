> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Qalam, Verse 42 (68:42) - Translation
> ˹Beware of˺ the Day the Shin ˹of Allah˺ will be bared,[^1] and the wicked will be asked to prostrate, but they will not be able to do so,
^translation

[^1]: Like the Face and the Hands, the Shin is believed by many to be one of the qualities of Allah, in a way befitting His Majesty and Greatness. Since baring the shin in the Arab culture is associated with the heat of battle, some interpret the verse metaphorically, so the meaning would be: “˹Beware of˺ the Day when horror sets in.”

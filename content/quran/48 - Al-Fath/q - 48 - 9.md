> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Fath, Verse 9 (48:9) - Translation
> so that you ˹believers˺ may have faith in Allah and His Messenger, support and honour him, and glorify Allah morning and evening.[^1] 
^translation

[^1]: Another possible translation: “… so that you ˹all˺ may believe in Allah and His Messenger, support ˹His cause˺, revere, and glorify Him morning and evening.”

> [!translation]+ Surah Qaf, Verse 14 (50:14) - Translation
> the residents of the Forest,[^1] and the people of Tubba’.[^2] Each rejected ˹their˺ messenger, so My warning was fulfilled.
^translation

[^1]: The people of Shu’aib (ﷺ).
[^2]: Tubba’ Al-Ḥimiari was an ancient righteous Yemeni king whose people persisted in disbelief and were destroyed, although they were superior to the Meccans in strength and manpower.

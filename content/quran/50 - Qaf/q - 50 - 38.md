> [!translation]+ Surah Qaf, Verse 38 (50:38) - Translation
> Indeed, We created the heavens and the earth and everything in between in six Days,[^1] and We were not ˹even˺ touched with fatigue.[^2]
^translation

[^1]: See footnote for [[q - 7 - 54|7:54]].
[^2]: Some scholars believe this verse comes in response to Exodus [[q - 31 - 17|31:17]], which says, “The Lord made the heavens and the earth in six days, but on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.”

> [!translation]+ Surah Sad, Verse 34 (38:34) - Translation
> And indeed, We tested Solomon, placing a ˹deformed˺ body on his throne,[^1] then he turned ˹to Allah in repentance˺.
^translation

[^1]: It is reported in a ḥadîth collected by Bukhâri that one night Solomon said that each of his wives would conceive a boy who would struggle in Allah’s cause. He forgot to say ‘Allah willing,” so only one of them gave birth to a deformed, dead baby. So Solomon prayed to Allah for forgiveness.

> [!translation]+ Surah Sad, Verse 8 (38:8) - Translation
> Has the Reminder been revealed ˹only˺ to him out of ˹all of˺ us?” In fact, they are ˹only˺ in doubt of My ˹revealed˺ Reminder.[^1] In fact, ˹they do so because˺ they have not yet tasted My punishment.
^translation

[^1]: In other words, they do not question the honesty of the Prophet (ﷺ), but question the Quran itself. This verse is similar to [[q - 6 - 33|6:33]].

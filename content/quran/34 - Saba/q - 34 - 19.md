> [!translation]+ Surah Saba, Verse 19 (34:19) - Translation
> But they said, “Our Lord! Make ˹the distances of˺ our journeys longer,” wronging themselves.[^1] So We reduced them to ˹cautionary˺ tales, and scattered them utterly. Surely in this are lessons for whoever is steadfast, grateful.
^translation

[^1]: They became bored of living comfortably and traveling easily. A similar example can be found in [[q - 2 - 61|2:61]], where the Children of Israel became bored with the manna and quails and wanted to eat other things such as onions and garlic.

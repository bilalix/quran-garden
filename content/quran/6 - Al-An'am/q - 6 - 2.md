> [!translation]+ Surah Al-An'am, Verse 2 (6:2) - Translation
> He is the One Who created you from clay,[^1] then appointed a term ˹for your death˺ and another known only to Him ˹for your resurrection˺—yet you continue to doubt!
^translation

[^1]: i.e., created your father, Adam, from clay. Some scholars suggest that if we examine all the minerals found in the earth (potassium, nitrogen, and carbon), we find that these are the same minerals that make up our body. Furthermore when we die, our bodies are absorbed back into the ground. And when we are resurrected, we will be resurrected from the ground (see [[q - 20 - 55|20:55]]).

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-An'am, Verse 103 (6:103) - Translation
> No vision can encompass Him, but He encompasses all vision. For He is the Most Subtle, All-Aware.[^1] 
^translation

[^1]: No one is able to see Allah in this world, but there is extensive evidence in the Quran and the teachings of the Prophet (ﷺ) that the believers will be able to see their Lord on the Day of Judgment.

> [!translation]+ Surah Az-Zumar, Verse 3 (39:3) - Translation
> Indeed, sincere devotion is due ˹only˺ to Allah. As for those who take other lords besides Him, ˹saying,˺ “We worship them only so they may bring us closer to Allah,” surely Allah will judge between all[^1] regarding what they differed about. Allah certainly does not guide whoever persists in lying and disbelief.
^translation

[^1]: Those who are devoted to Allah alone and those who associate other gods with Him.

> [!translation]+ Surah Az-Zumar, Verse 29 (39:29) - Translation
> Allah sets forth the parable of a slave owned by several quarrelsome masters, and a slave owned by only one master. Are they equal in condition?[^1] Praise be to Allah! In fact, most of them do not know.
^translation

[^1]: The slave owned by several quarrelsome masters will always be confused because their masters will always give them conflicting orders—similarly, one who worships multiple gods will never find peace between them. This parable is a logical argument against the existence of multiple gods, since each god would try to covet what it created. See [[q - 23 - 91|23:91]] for a similar argument.

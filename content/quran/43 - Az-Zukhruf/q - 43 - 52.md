> [!translation]+ Surah Az-Zukhruf, Verse 52 (43:52) - Translation
> Am I not better than this nobody who can hardly express himself?[^1]
^translation

[^1]: Moses used to have a speech impediment. But when he became a prophet he prayed to Allah to help him speak clearly, and his prayer was answered (see [20:25-36](/20:25-36)).

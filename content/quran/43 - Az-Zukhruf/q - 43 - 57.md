> [!translation]+ Surah Az-Zukhruf, Verse 57 (43:57) - Translation
> When the son of Mary was cited as an example ˹in argument˺, your people ˹O Prophet˺ broke into ˹joyful˺ applause.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: When [[q - 21 - 98|21:98]] was revealed, warning the polytheists that all false gods will be in Hell, ’Abdullâh ibn Az-Ziba’ra, a poet who always attacked Islam, argued with the Prophet (ﷺ) that if what the verse says is true, then Jesus will be in Hell as well! The polytheists, who were present, laughed in agreement with this argument. The Prophet (ﷺ) replied that the verse was talking exclusively about idols, adding that Jesus himself did not ask anyone to worship him. Verse [21:101](/21:101) was later revealed in support of the Prophet’s argument. Eventually, ’Abdullâh accepted Islam.

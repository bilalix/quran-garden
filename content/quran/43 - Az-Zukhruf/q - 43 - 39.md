> [!translation]+ Surah Az-Zukhruf, Verse 39 (43:39) - Translation
> ˹It will be said to both,˺ “Since you all did wrong, sharing in the punishment will be of no benefit to you this Day.”[^1] 
^translation

[^1]: From a worldly perspective, many people are comforted when they hear of others who went through similar trials as them. But this will not be the case on Judgment Day. Everyone will be desperate to save themselves from the punishment, regardless of others.

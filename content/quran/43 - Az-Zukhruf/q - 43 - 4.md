> [!translation]+ Surah Az-Zukhruf, Verse 4 (43:4) - Translation
> And indeed, it is—in the Master Record[^1] with Us—highly esteemed, rich in wisdom.
^translation

[^1]: This refers to the Preserved Tablet, Al-Lawḥ Al-Maḥfûẓ, in which Allah has written the destiny of His entire creation.

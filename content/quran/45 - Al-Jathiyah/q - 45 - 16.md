> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Jathiyah, Verse 16 (45:16) - Translation
> Indeed, We gave the Children of Israel the Scripture, wisdom, and prophethood; granted them good, lawful provisions; and favoured them above the others.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: By sending many prophets, and showing them unique miracles such as the splitting of the sea.

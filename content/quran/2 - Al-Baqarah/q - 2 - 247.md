> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 247 (2:247) - Translation
> Their prophet told them, “Allah has appointed Saul[^1] to be your king.” They protested, “How can he be our king when some of us are more deserving of kingship than he, and he has not been blessed with vast riches?” He replied, “Allah has chosen him over you and blessed him with knowledge and stature. Allah grants kingship to whoever He wills. And Allah is All-Bountiful, All-Knowing.”
^translation

[^1]: Ṭalûṭ.

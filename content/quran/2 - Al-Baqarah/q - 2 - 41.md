> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 41 (2:41) - Translation
> Believe in My revelations which confirm your Scriptures.[^1] Do not be the first to deny them or trade them for a fleeting gain.[^2] And be mindful of Me.
^translation

[^1]: This refers to the passages in the Torah and the Gospel that call for the worship of the One True God, doing what is good, and avoiding what is evil.
[^2]: Trading Allah’s revelations for a fleeting gain is a recurring theme in the first few sûrahs of the Quran. This refers to the practice of some Jewish authorities in Medina who contradicted certain rulings in the Torah by giving people lenient opinions only to please them in exchange for money.

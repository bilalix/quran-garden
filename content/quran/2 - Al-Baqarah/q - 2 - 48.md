> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 48 (2:48) - Translation
> Guard yourselves against the Day on which no soul will be of help to another. No intercession[^1] will be accepted, no ransom taken, and no help will be given.
^translation

[^1]: “Intercession” is the act of pleading with Allah on behalf of another person on the Day of Judgment.

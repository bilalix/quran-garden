> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 43 (2:43) - Translation
> Establish prayer, pay alms-tax,[^1] and bow down with those who bow down.
^translation

[^1]: The alms-tax (zakâh) is the payment of 2.5% of someone’s savings only if the amount is equivalent to or greater than 85 g of gold, if that amount remains untouched for a whole Islamic year—around 355 days.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 37 (2:37) - Translation
> Then Adam was inspired with words ˹of prayer˺ by his Lord,[^1] so He accepted his repentance. Surely He is the Accepter of Repentance, Most Merciful.
^translation

[^1]: The prayer that Adam and Eve were inspired to say is mentioned in [[q - 7 - 23|7:23]], “Our Lord! We have wronged ourselves. If You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will certainly be losers.”

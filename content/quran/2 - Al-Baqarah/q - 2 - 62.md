> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 62 (2:62) - Translation
> Indeed, the believers, Jews, Christians, and Sabians[^1]—whoever ˹truly˺ believes in Allah and the Last Day and does good will have their reward with their Lord. And there will be no fear for them, nor will they grieve.[^2] 
^translation

[^1]: The Sabians are an indigenous group that believes in a supreme being and lives mostly in Iraq.
[^2]: This verse should be understood in light of [[q - 3 - 19|3:19]] and [3:85](/3:85). For more details, see the Introduction.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 67 (2:67) - Translation
> And ˹remember˺ when Moses said to his people, “Allah commands you to sacrifice a cow.”[^1] They replied, “Are you mocking us?” Moses responded, “I seek refuge in Allah from acting foolishly!”
^translation

[^1]: This sûrah is named after the cow in this story, which happened at the time of Moses (ﷺ). A rich man was killed by his nephew, his only heir, and the body was thrown at the door of an innocent man. After a long investigation, no one was identified as the killer. Moses (ﷺ) prayed for guidance and was told that the only way to find the killer was to sacrifice a cow and strike the victim with a piece of it. When this was done, the victim spoke miraculously and said who the killer was.

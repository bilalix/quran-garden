> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 158 (2:158) - Translation
> Indeed, ˹the hills of˺ Ṣafa and Marwah[^1] are among the symbols of Allah. So whoever performs the pilgrimage or minor pilgrimage,[^2] let them walk between ˹the two hills˺. And whoever does good willingly, Allah is truly Appreciative, All-Knowing.
^translation

[^1]: Ṣafa and Marwah are two hills near the Ka’bah in Mecca.
[^2]: Known as ḥajj and ’umrah to the Sacred Mosque in Mecca.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 190 (2:190) - Translation
> Fight in the cause of Allah ˹only˺ against those who wage war against you, but do not exceed the limits.[^1] Allah does not like transgressors.
^translation

[^1]: “Do not exceed the limits” refers to Islamic warfare guidelines set by the Prophet. In an authentic saying collected by Abu Dawûd, he (ﷺ) is reported to have instructed the Muslim army, “Depart in the Name of Allah and with His help—following the way of the Messenger of Allah. Do not kill an old man, a child, or a woman. Do not mutilate dead bodies of the enemy. Be gracious and courteous, for Allah loves those who act with grace.” The Prophet (ﷺ) also says, “Do not wish to meet your enemy in battle but always pray for well-being. If fighting is a must, then be steadfast.”

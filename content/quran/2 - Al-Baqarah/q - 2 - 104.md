> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 104 (2:104) - Translation
> O believers! Do not say, “Râ’ina.” [Herd us!] But say, “Unẓurna,” [Tend to us!] and listen ˹attentively˺.[^1] And the disbelievers will suffer a painful punishment.
^translation

[^1]: Some of the disbelievers used to play with words when they addressed the Prophet (ﷺ) in order to ridicule him. So instead of saying, râ’ina “listen to us,” they would say, râ’îna “our shepherd” or “the foolish among us.” They would say loudly, “We listen,” then whisper, “but we disobey!” and say, “Hear us,” then, “may you never hear!” They used to say to each other, “If he had truly been a prophet of Allah, he would have known that we are mocking him.” Therefore, this verse (along with

  
[4:46](/4:46)) was revealed commanding the believers to avoid such words altogether. Better words are recommended.

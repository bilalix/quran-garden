> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 88 (2:88) - Translation
> They say, “Our hearts are unreceptive!”[^1] In fact, Allah has condemned them for their disbelief. They have but little faith.
^translation

[^1]: Their hearts are unreceptive because they claim they have enough knowledge already.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 115 (2:115) - Translation
> To Allah belong the east and the west, so wherever you turn you are facing ˹towards˺ Allah.[^1] Surely Allah is All-Encompassing,[^2] All-Knowing.
^translation

[^1]: lit., wherever you turn, there is the Face of Allah.
[^2]: His mercy and knowledge covers all.

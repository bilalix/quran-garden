> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 238 (2:238) - Translation
> Observe the ˹five obligatory˺ prayers—especially the middle prayer[^1]—and stand in true devotion to Allah.
^translation

[^1]: As mentioned in several authentic sayings of Prophet Muḥammad (ﷺ), the middle prayer is the afternoon prayer, ’Aṣr.

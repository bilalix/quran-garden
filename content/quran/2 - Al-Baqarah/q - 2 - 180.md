> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 180 (2:180) - Translation
> It is prescribed that when death approaches any of you—if they leave something of value—a will should be made in favour of parents and immediate family with fairness.[^1] ˹This is˺ an obligation on those who are mindful ˹of Allah˺.
^translation

[^1]: This ruling should be understood in light of the inheritance laws in [4:11-12](/4:11-12), which give specific shares to parents and close relatives. Relatives who do not have a share may get a bequest up to one third of the estate.

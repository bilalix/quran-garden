> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 34 (2:34) - Translation
> And ˹remember˺ when We said to the angels, “Prostrate before Adam,”[^1] so they all did—but not Iblîs,[^2] who refused and acted arrogantly,[^3] becoming unfaithful.
^translation

[^1]: Prostration in this verse does not mean worship, but an act of respect. A similar occurrence can be found in Sûrah 12, where Jacob (ﷺ), his wife, and his eleven children knelt down before Joseph (ﷺ).
[^2]: Iblîs was the name of Satan before his fall from grace. Iblîs was not an angel, but one of the jinn (see [[q - 18 - 50|18:50]]). Jinn are another creation of Allah, similar to humans in that they—unlike angels—have free will, but are made of smokeless fire and live in another plane of existence.
[^3]: The command to prostrate was a test of obedience. Satan arrogantly refused to comply because he believed he was superior to Adam—arguing that Adam was made of mud while Satan was made of fire (see [[q - 7 - 12|7:12]]).

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 234 (2:234) - Translation
> As for those of you who die and leave widows behind, let them observe a waiting period of four months and ten days. When they have reached the end of this period, then you[^1] are not accountable for what they decide for themselves in a reasonable manner. And Allah is All-Aware of what you do.
^translation

[^1]: i.e., the guardians.

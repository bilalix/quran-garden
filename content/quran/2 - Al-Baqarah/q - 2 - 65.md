> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 65 (2:65) - Translation
> You are already aware of those of you who broke the Sabbath. We said to them, “Be disgraced apes!”[^1]
^translation

[^1]: Although many scholars believe that these individuals were turned into real apes, others interpret this verse in a metaphorical sense. This style is not uncommon in the Quran. See [[q - 62 - 5|62:5]] regarding the donkey that carries books and [2:18](/2:18) regarding the deaf, dumb, and blind.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Fajr, Verse 1 (89:1) - Translation
> By the dawn,[^1]
^translation

[^1]: Like many sûrahs, this one begins with an oath from Allah. He has the right to swear by any object of His creation (i.e., the sun, the moon, the stars, the dawn, or the angels). As for us, we are only allowed to swear by Allah alone.

> [!translation]+ Surah Ali 'Imran, Verse 193 (3:193) - Translation
> Our Lord! We have heard the caller[^1] to ˹true˺ belief, ˹proclaiming,˺ ‘Believe in your Lord ˹alone˺,’ so we believed. Our Lord! Forgive our sins, absolve us of our misdeeds, and allow us ˹each˺ to die as one of the virtuous.
^translation

[^1]: The caller is Muḥammad (ﷺ).

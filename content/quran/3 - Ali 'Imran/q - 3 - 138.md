> [!translation]+ Surah Ali 'Imran, Verse 138 (3:138) - Translation
> This[^1] is an insight to humanity—a guide and a lesson to the God-fearing.
^translation

[^1]: “This” either refers to how the forces of evil are ultimately destroyed, or that the Quran is a reminder of the destruction of evildoers.

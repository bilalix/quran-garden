> [!translation]+ Surah Ali 'Imran, Verse 14 (3:14) - Translation
> The enjoyment of ˹worldly˺ desires—women, children,[^1] treasures of gold and silver, fine horses, cattle, and fertile land—has been made appealing to people. These are the pleasures of this worldly life, but with Allah is the finest destination.
^translation

[^1]: Banîn means sons. In the ancient Arab culture, sons were a source of pride for their parents and tribes. This is because they provided for their families and took up arms in defence of their tribes.

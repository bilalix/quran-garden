> [!translation]+ Surah Ali 'Imran, Verse 36 (3:36) - Translation
> When she delivered, she said, “My Lord! I have given birth to a girl,”—and Allah fully knew what she had delivered—“and the male is not like the female.[^1] I have named her Mary, and I seek Your protection for her and her offspring from Satan, the accursed.”[^2]
^translation

[^1]: Service at the temple was hosted by males only.
[^2]: The prayers of Mary’s mother were answered. In a ḥadîth collected by Bukhâri and Muslim, the Prophet (ﷺ) says, “Every child is touched by Satan when they are born—and they cry because of this contact—except Jesus and his mother.”

> [!translation]+ Surah Ali 'Imran, Verse 200 (3:200) - Translation
> O believers! Patiently endure, persevere, stand on guard,[^1] and be mindful of Allah, so you may be successful.
^translation

[^1]: Râbiṭo refers to watching over the borders of one’s homeland. It can also mean keeping up one’s prayers.

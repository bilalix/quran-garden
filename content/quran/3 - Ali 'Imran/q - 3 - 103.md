> [!translation]+ Surah Ali 'Imran, Verse 103 (3:103) - Translation
> And hold firmly to the rope of Allah[^1] and do not be divided. Remember Allah’s favour upon you when you were enemies, then He united your hearts, so you—by His grace—became brothers. And you were at the brink of a fiery pit and He saved you from it. This is how Allah makes His revelations clear to you, so that you may be ˹rightly˺ guided.
^translation

[^1]: i.e., Allah’s covenant.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Hashr, Verse 15 (59:15) - Translation
> They are ˹both just˺ like those who recently went down before them:[^1] they tasted the evil consequences of their doings. And they will suffer a painful punishment.
^translation

[^1]: This either refers to the defeat of the Meccan pagans at the Battle of Badr or the exile of Banu Qainuqâ’.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Isra, Verse 60 (17:60) - Translation
> And ˹remember, O Prophet˺ when We told you, “Certainly your Lord encompasses the people.” And We have made what We brought you to see[^1] as well as the cursed tree ˹mentioned˺ in the Quran[^2] only as a test for the people. We keep warning them, but it only increases them greatly in defiance.
^translation

[^1]: During the Night Journey mentioned in [[q - 17 - 1|17:1]].
[^2]: The tree of Zaqqûm which grows in the depths of Hell as mentioned in [37:62-65](/37:62-65). The pagans of Mecca used to make fun of the Prophet (ﷺ) and say, “How can a tree grow in Hell?”

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Isra, Verse 13 (17:13) - Translation
> We have bound every human’s destiny to their neck.[^1] And on the Day of Judgment We will bring forth to each ˹person˺ a record which they will find laid open.
^translation

[^1]: This refers to the deeds that one is destined to do of their own free will. Out of His infinite knowledge, Allah knows people’s choices even before they make them. In the Hereafter, He will raise them from the dead for judgment.

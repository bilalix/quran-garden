> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Isra, Verse 56 (17:56) - Translation
> Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Invoke those you claim ˹to be divine˺[^1] besides Him—they do not have the power to undo harm from you or transfer it ˹to someone else˺.”
^translation

[^1]: i.e., Jesus, Ezra, and the angels. Idol worship is refuted in other passages such as [[q - 7 - 22|7:22]] and [34:22](/34:22).

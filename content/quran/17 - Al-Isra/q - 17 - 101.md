> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Isra, Verse 101 (17:101) - Translation
> We surely gave Moses nine clear signs.[^1] ˹You, O Prophet, can˺ ask the Children of Israel. When Moses came to them, Pharaoh said to him, “I really think that you, O Moses, are bewitched.”
^translation

[^1]: The nine signs of Moses are: the staff, the hand (both mentioned in [20:17-22](/20:17-22)), famine, shortage of crops, floods, locusts, lice, frogs, and blood (all mentioned in [7:130-133](/7:130-133)). These signs came as proofs for Pharaoh and the Egyptians. Otherwise, Moses had some other signs such water gushing out of the rock after he hit it with his staff, and splitting the sea.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Insan, Verse 2 (76:2) - Translation
> ˹For˺ indeed, We ˹alone˺ created humans from a drop of mixed fluids,[^1] ˹in order˺ to test them, so We made them hear and see.
^translation

[^1]: The mixture of male and female gametes (sperm and egg) which form the zygote after fertilization.

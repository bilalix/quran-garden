> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Insan, Verse 26 (76:26) - Translation
> and prostrate before Him during part of the night,[^1] and glorify Him long at night.[^2] 
^translation

[^1]: This verse refers to the five daily prayers: the dawn prayer, then noon and afternoon prayers, then sunset and late evening prayers, respectively.
[^2]: Extra prayers offered at night. See [[q - 73 - 20|73:20]].

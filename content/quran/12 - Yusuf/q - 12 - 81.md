> [!translation]+ Surah Yusuf, Verse 81 (12:81) - Translation
> Return to your father and say, ‘O our father! Your son committed theft. We testify only to what we know. We could not guard against the unforeseen.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: We did not know when we gave you our solemn oath that our brother was going to steal.

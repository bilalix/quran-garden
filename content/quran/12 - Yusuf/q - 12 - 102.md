> [!translation]+ Surah Yusuf, Verse 102 (12:102) - Translation
> That is from the stories of the unseen which We reveal to you ˹O Prophet˺. You were not present when they ˹all˺[^1] made up their minds, and when they plotted ˹against Joseph˺.
^translation

[^1]: This includes Joseph’s brothers, the travellers who picked him up from the well and sold him into slavery, and the Chief Minister’s wife and other women in the city.

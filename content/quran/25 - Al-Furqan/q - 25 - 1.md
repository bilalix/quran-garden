> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Furqan, Verse 1 (25:1) - Translation
> Blessed is the One Who sent down the Standard[^1] to His servant,[^2] so that he may be a warner to the whole world.[^3]
^translation

[^1]: Al-Furqân, which is one of names of the Quran, means "the standard to distinguish between right and wrong."
[^2]: Prophet Muḥammad (ﷺ).
[^3]: For both humans and jinn.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Hijr, Verse 72 (15:72) - Translation
> By your life ˹O Prophet˺,[^1] they certainly wandered blindly, intoxicated ˹by lust˺.
^translation

[^1]: This is the only time in the Quran where Allah swears by the life of a human being. Elsewhere Allah swears by the sun, the moon, the stars, and other marvels of His creation.

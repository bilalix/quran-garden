> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Hijr, Verse 47 (15:47) - Translation
> We will remove whatever bitterness they had in their hearts.[^1] In a friendly manner, they will be on thrones, facing one another.
^translation

[^1]: i.e., the bitterness they had in their hearts towards other believers who wronged them in the worldly life.

> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nahl, Verse 59 (16:59) - Translation
> He hides himself from the people because of the bad news he has received. Should he keep her in disgrace, or bury her ˹alive˺ in the ground? Evil indeed is their judgment![^1]
^translation

[^1]: Some pagan Arabs used to bury their infant daughters alive for fear of shame or poverty. This practice was condemned and abolished by Islam. See [[q - 6 - 151|6:151]] and [81:8-9](/81:8-9)

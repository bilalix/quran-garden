> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nahl, Verse 67 (16:67) - Translation
> And from the fruits of palm trees and grapevines you derive intoxicants[^1] as well as wholesome provision. Surely in this is a sign for those who understand.
^translation

[^1]: Intoxicants were later prohibited in the Quran in three stages: [[q - 2 - 19|2:19]], [4:43](/4:43), and finally [5:90-91](/5:90-91).

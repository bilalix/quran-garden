> [!translation]+ Surah Al-A'raf, Verse 175 (7:175) - Translation
> And relate to them ˹O Prophet˺ the story of the one to whom We gave Our signs, but he abandoned them, so Satan took hold of him, and he became a deviant.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: Bal’am ibn Ba’ûrâ' was a scholar who lived at the time of Moses (ﷺ) but later deviated from the truth.

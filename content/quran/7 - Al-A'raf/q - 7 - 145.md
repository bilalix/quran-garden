> [!translation]+ Surah Al-A'raf, Verse 145 (7:145) - Translation
> We wrote for him on the Tablets ˹the fundamentals˺ of everything; commandments and explanations of all things. ˹We commanded,˺ “Hold to this firmly and ask your people to take the best of it.[^1] I will soon show ˹all of˺ you the home of the rebellious.[^2]
^translation

[^1]: To follow the commandments that generate more rewards than others, to put grace before justice, etc.
[^2]: This could either mean the ruins of destroyed nations or the Hellfire which is the home of the wicked.

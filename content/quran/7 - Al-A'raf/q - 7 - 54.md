> [!translation]+ Surah Al-A'raf, Verse 54 (7:54) - Translation
> Indeed your Lord is Allah Who created the heavens and the earth in six Days,[^1] then established Himself on the Throne. He makes the day and night overlap in rapid succession. He created the sun, the moon, and the stars—all subjected by His command. The creation and the command belong to Him ˹alone˺. Blessed is Allah—Lord of all worlds!
^translation

[^1]: The word day is not always used in the Quran to mean a 24-hour period. According to [[q - 22 - 47|22:47]], a heavenly Day is 1000 years of our time. The Day of Judgment will be 50 000 years of our time (see [70:4](/70:4)). Hence, the six Days of creation refer to six eons of time, known only by Allah.

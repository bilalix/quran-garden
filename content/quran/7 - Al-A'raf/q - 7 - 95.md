> [!translation]+ Surah Al-A'raf, Verse 95 (7:95) - Translation
> Then We changed their adversity to prosperity until they flourished and argued ˹falsely˺, “Our forefathers ˹too˺ had been visited by adversity and prosperity.”[^1] So We seized them by surprise, while they were unaware.
^translation

[^1]: They argued that life has its ups and downs and, therefore, they did not perceive adversity as a punishment or prosperity as a test—arguing that the same thing happened to their ancestors.

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-A'raf, Verse 171 (7:171) - Translation
> And ˹remember˺ when We raised the mountain over them as if it were a cloud and they thought it would fall on them.[^1] ˹We said,˺ “Hold firmly to that ˹Scripture˺ which We have given you and observe its teachings so perhaps you will become mindful ˹of Allah˺.”
^translation

[^1]: The mountain was raised over their heads when Moses came to them with the teachings of the Torah and was met with defiance. So the mountain was raised as a miracle and a display of Allah’s infinite power over them.

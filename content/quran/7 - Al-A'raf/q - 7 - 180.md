> [!translation]+ Surah Al-A'raf, Verse 180 (7:180) - Translation
> Allah has the Most Beautiful Names. So call upon Him by them, and keep away from those who abuse His Names.[^1] They will be punished for what they used to do.
^translation

[^1]: Those who twist Allah’s Names then use them to call false gods. For example, Allât, a name of one of the idols was derived from Allah (the One God), Al-’Uzza was derived from Al-’Azîz (the Almighty), and Manât was derived from Al-Mannân (the Bestower).

> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Qasas, Verse 85 (28:85) - Translation
> Most certainly, the One Who has ordained the Quran for you will ˹ultimately˺ bring you back home ˹to Mecca˺.[^1] Say, “My Lord knows best who has come with ˹true˺ guidance and who is clearly astray.”
^translation

[^1]: This verse was revealed while the Prophet (ﷺ) was on the way to Medina, emigrating from Mecca after years of persecution. Eventually the Prophet (ﷺ) returned to Mecca and the majority of its people accepted Islam.

> [!translation]+ Surah As-Sajdah, Verse 23 (32:23) - Translation
> Indeed, We gave the Scripture to Moses—so let there be no doubt ˹O Prophet˺ that you ˹too˺ are receiving revelations—[^1]and We made it a guide for the Children of Israel.
^translation

[^1]: Other possible translations: 1\. “Do not be in doubt that Moses received it.” 2\. “Do not be in doubt of your meeting with Moses.”

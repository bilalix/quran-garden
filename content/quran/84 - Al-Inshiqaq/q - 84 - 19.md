> [!translation]+ Surah Al-Inshiqaq, Verse 19 (84:19) - Translation
> You will certainly pass from one state to another.[^1]
^translation

[^1]: You will pass from life to death and from death to resurrection, from well-being to sickness and from sickness to well-being, from poverty to richness and from richness to poverty, etc.

> [!translation]+ Surah Maryam, Verse 26 (19:26) - Translation
> So eat and drink, and put your heart at ease. But if you see any of the people, say, ‘I have vowed silence[^1] to the Most Compassionate, so I am not talking to anyone today.’”
^translation

[^1]: lit., fast ˹from speech˺. Linguistically, ṣawm means to abstain from something, such as food, speaking, and playing.

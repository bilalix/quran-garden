> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nisa, Verse 116 (4:116) - Translation
> Surely Allah does not forgive associating ˹others˺ with Him ˹in worship˺,[^1] but forgives anything else of whoever He wills. Indeed, whoever associates ˹others˺ with Allah has clearly gone far astray.
^translation

[^1]: This applies to those who die in a state of disbelief in Allah. But those who repent before their death and mend their ways, their repentance will be accepted (see [25:68-70](/25:68-70)).

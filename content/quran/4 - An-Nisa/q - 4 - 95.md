> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nisa, Verse 95 (4:95) - Translation
> Those who stay at home—except those with valid excuses[^1]—are not equal to those who strive in the cause of Allah with their wealth and their lives. Allah has elevated in rank those who strive with their wealth and their lives above those who stay behind ˹with valid excuses˺. Allah has promised each a fine reward, but those who strive will receive a far better reward than others—
^translation

[^1]: This includes women, the elderly, the sick, etc.

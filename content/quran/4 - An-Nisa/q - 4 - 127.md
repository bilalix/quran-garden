> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nisa, Verse 127 (4:127) - Translation
> They ask you ˹O Prophet˺ regarding women. Say, “It is Allah Who instructs you regarding them. Instruction has ˹already˺ been revealed in the Book[^1] concerning the orphan women you deprive of their due rights[^2] but still wish to marry, also helpless children, as well as standing up for orphans’ rights. And whatever good you do is certainly well known to Allah.”
^translation

[^1]: This refers to verses [V.4:2-11](/4:2-11) of this sûrah.
[^2]: i.e., their inheritance and dowries.

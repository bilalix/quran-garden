> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nisa, Verse 101 (4:101) - Translation
> When you travel through the land, it is permissible for you to shorten the prayer[^1]—˹especially˺ if you fear an attack by the disbelievers. Indeed, the disbelievers are your sworn enemies.
^translation

[^1]: Generally, it is permissible for Muslims who are travelling a distance of 85 km or more to shorten their prayers. A four-unit prayer is reduced to two.

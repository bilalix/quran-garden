> [!translation]+ Surah An-Nisa, Verse 148 (4:148) - Translation
> Allah does not like negative thoughts to be voiced—except by those who have been wronged.[^1] Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing.
^translation

[^1]: i.e., Allah does not like talking about people behind their backs—except when someone who has been wronged seeks counselling or assistance from authorities.

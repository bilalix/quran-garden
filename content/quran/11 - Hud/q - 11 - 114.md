> [!translation]+ Surah Hud, Verse 114 (11:114) - Translation
> Establish prayer ˹O Prophet˺ at both ends of the day and in the early part of the night.[^1] Surely good deeds wipe out evil deeds. That is a reminder for the mindful.
^translation

[^1]: This verse talks about the five daily prayers. The prayers at the two ends of the day are the dawn prayer (Fajr) at one end and the afternoon prayers (Ẓuhr and ’Aṣr) at the other. Prayers in the early part of the night include the sunset (Maghrib) and late evening (’Ishâ') prayers.

> And his wife was standing by, so she laughed,[^1] then We gave her good news of ˹the birth of˺ Isaac, and, after him, Jacob.
^translation

[^1]: She laughed after her husband was assured that the guests intended no harm or when she heard the news of the imminent destruction of the sinful people of Lot.

> [!translation]+ Surah Hud, Verse 56 (11:56) - Translation
> I have put my trust in Allah—my Lord and your Lord. There is no living creature that is not completely under His control.[^1] Surely my Lord’s Way is perfect justice.[^2]
^translation

[^1]: lit., there is no moving creature that He does not hold by its forelock.
[^2]: lit., my Lord is on the Straight Path.

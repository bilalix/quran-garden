> [!translation]+ Surah Hud, Verse 77 (11:77) - Translation
> When Our messenger-angels came to Lot, he was distressed and worried by their arrival.[^1] He said, “This is a terrible day.”
^translation

[^1]: Since the angels came in the form of handsome men, Lot was worried for the dignity and safety of his guests among his people—not knowing that they were angels.

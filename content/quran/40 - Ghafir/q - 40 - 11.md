> [!translation]+ Surah Ghafir, Verse 11 (40:11) - Translation
> They will plead, “Our Lord! You made us lifeless twice, and gave us life twice.[^1] Now we confess our sins. So is there any way out?”
^translation

[^1]: You created us from nothing, then gave us life in the wombs of our mothers, then caused us to die at the end of our worldly lives, and finally resurrected us after our death (see [[q - 2 - 28|2:28]]).

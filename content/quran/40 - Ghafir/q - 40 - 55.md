> [!translation]+ Surah Ghafir, Verse 55 (40:55) - Translation
> So be patient ˹O Prophet˺, ˹for˺ Allah’s promise is certainly true. Seek forgiveness for your shortcomings.[^1] And glorify the praises of your Lord morning and evening.
^translation

[^1]: Like other prophets, Muḥammad (ﷺ) was infallible of sin. The verse here refers to misjudgments, such as the Prophet’s response to the blind man ([80:1-10](/80:1-10)), Jonah’s departure from his city without Allah’s permission ([21:87-88](/21:87-88)), and David’s suspicions of the two men who scaled the wall of his sanctuary ([38:21-25](/38:21-25)). If the Prophet (ﷺ) himself is urged to seek forgiveness, then the believers are even more in need of praying for Allah’s forgiveness.

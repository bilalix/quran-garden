> [!translation]+ Surah At-Tahrim, Verse 10 (66:10) - Translation
> Allah sets forth an example for the disbelievers: the wife of Noah and the wife of Lot. Each was married to one of Our righteous servants, yet betrayed them.[^1] So their husbands were of no benefit to them against Allah whatsoever. Both were told, “Enter the Fire, along with the others!”
^translation

[^1]: Both wives were disbelievers. Noah’s wife used to give away the identity of the new believers so the disbelievers could persecute them. And Lot’s wife used to tell the men about her husband’s handsome male visitors so they could approach them.
